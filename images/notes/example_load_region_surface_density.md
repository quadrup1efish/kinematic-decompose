# Reading and limitations

- Same spatial slab and orientation in all three square panels.
- Independent logarithmic color scales (positive-pixel 1st/99.9th percentiles).
- Read extended filaments from DM/gas; do not infer inflow from density alone.
- Stars use a one-pixel kernel to avoid Mpc-sized sparse-neighbour blobs.
- Gas is an SPH visualization of cell masses, not an exact Voronoi projection.
- PartType4 is shown without an additional wind-particle cut.
- Finite slab boundaries truncate kernels; pixel sampling also affects mass integrals.
- The figure demonstrates region loading, not quantitative filament identification.
